Sample output — Premix Handling SOP
Auto-generated · Beginner-friendly · 6 steps
1
Verify premix delivery
Check the delivery bill against Purchase Order. Ensure batch number, FSSAI approval code, and expiry date are printed on packaging.
2
Store at correct conditions
Store premix in a dry, cool room (<30°C). Keep bags off the floor on pallets. Label storage date on each bag using the provided stickers.
3
Log batch in register
Enter batch ID, quantity (kg), supplier name, and receipt date in the Batch Register (Form FF-03). Keep a digital copy on the field app.
4
Mix at correct ratio
Add 1 kg premix per 100 kg of wheat flour. Use the calibrated weigh scale only. Never estimate by volume.
5
Sample for lab testing
Collect 250g sample per batch. Label with date, mill code, and batch ID. Submit to district lab within 48 hours of production.
6
Report non-conformance
If lab result fails, isolate the batch immediately. Notify District Food Safety Officer within 24 hours using Form FF-05.
